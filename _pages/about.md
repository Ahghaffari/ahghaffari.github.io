I am a doctoral researcher in Computer Science and Engineering at the [Future Computing Group (FCG)](https://ubicomp.oulu.fi/research/fcg), [Centre for Applied Computing](https://ubicomp.oulu.fi/), Faculty of Information Technology and Electrical Engineering (ITEE), [University of Oulu](https://www.oulu.fi/), Finland, supervised by Adj. Prof. Ekaterina Gilman and Adj. Prof. Susanna Pirttikangas. I develop graph-based machine learning methods that turn heterogeneous real-world data into reliable predictions and decision support for cities and mobility systems.

My PhD focuses on **spatio-temporal graph neural networks (STGNNs)** for urban prediction, combining ideas from machine learning, network science, and urban analytics. A central theme of my work is how raw spatio-temporal data should be mapped into graphs, and how model design can make graph learning more accurate and robust. This line of work includes road-aware graph construction (STRAM, *Neurocomputing*), the open-source STM-Graph framework (CIKM 2025), and mixture-of-experts GNNs for traffic forecasting (IEEE MDM 2026), with further work on graph transformers and spatio-temporal model design in progress.

From September to November 2026, I am a visiting researcher with the [Machine Learning for Smart Mobility (MLSM)](https://mlsm.man.dtu.dk/) group at the [Technical University of Denmark (DTU)](https://www.dtu.dk/english/), hosted by Prof. Francisco Camara Pereira, where I work on GNN surrogates for traffic assignment.

Beyond GNNs, I work on **LLMs and agentic AI**, including adapting LLMs to online communities (RedditPersona, CIKM 2026), LLM-powered travel planning from mobility patterns (IEEE BigData 2025), and agentic systems built on knowledge graphs. I have also contributed to research on **federated and privacy-preserving learning**, smart city optimization, and anomaly detection in 5G networks.

Before my PhD, I spent over three years as a computer vision and deep learning researcher at [Fard Iran Co.](https://fardiran.com/en/), building production AI for road traffic: automatic number plate recognition, weigh-in-motion (WIM) vehicle weighing, intelligent surveillance, and driver-behavior analysis. I helped deploy and maintain these systems at more than 70 sites. In parallel, I developed deep learning models for air pollution prediction at the Institute of Water and Energy, Sharif University of Technology. I hold an M.Sc. from [Sharif University of Technology](http://www.en.sharif.edu/) and a B.Sc. from [Amirkabir University of Technology](https://www.aut.ac.ir/en).

I enjoy the full path from a research idea to a system people actually rely on. I am always happy to discuss research, collaboration, or new opportunities. Feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:amirhossein.ghaffari@oulu.fi) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/amirhosseinghaffari/).
